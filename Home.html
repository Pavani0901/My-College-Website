HISTORY OF PRAGNYA DEGREE COLLEGE

Pragnya Degree College was established in 2010 in Chandanagar, Hyderabad, Telangana. Key Historical & Institutional Details • Foundation Year: 2010 • Management & Affiliation: It is a private un-aided institution managed by the Pragnya Group of Colleges and affiliated with Osmania University. • Leadership: Founded and chaired by S. Srinivasa Reddy (M.Com, LLB). • Evolution & Offerings: Originally started as a focused degree college, it has expanded to offer co-educational and women's undergraduate and postgraduate programs—such as BBA, BCA, B.Com, B.Sc., M.Com, and M.Sc. in Computer Science—with a focus on affordable education, skill development, and industry readiness.
